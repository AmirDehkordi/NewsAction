# NA-06 decisions — zero-shot decision-model tournament and contamination probes

Date: 2026-10-26. Branch: `feat/NA-06-tournament` (worktree `NewsAction-NA05`, cut from `main` @ `dd40ca6`; NA-08
ran in parallel in its own worktree). Self-audit: `artifacts/NA-06/REPORT.md`. PREREG deviations: one row in §15

## Package layout (`src/newsaction/tournament/`)

- `__init__`: `configs/tournament.yaml` → `TournamentConfig`; paths; `log_trial` (NA-06 trials, never twice).
- `sample`: the val-A pool and the nested samples.
- `metrics`: daily Spearman IC, Newey–West summaries, paired differences, the 3-class ECE, Spearman with an exact
  permutation p-value for small n.
- `mcs`: the Hansen–Lunde–Nason Model Confidence Set (T_R and T_max), with a CLI for the verifier
  (`python -m newsaction.tournament.mcs --seed N`).
- `edits`: the counterfactual edit rules of probe c.
- `probes`: probe designs (company and month options, the shuffled-news derangement), scores, the PREREG §8.3
  screens, the reading-validity tables.
- `run`: the stages (`python -m newsaction.tournament.run --stage samples | dryrun1 | stage1 | select | dryrun2 |
  stage2 | validity | report | handoff`).

Every constant PREREG gives lives in `configs/tournament.yaml`, and `tests/test_tournament_{config,screens}.py`
compare it with the PREREG text (the screen thresholds are parsed out of §8.3's own table).

## Samples (S1)

- **Pool:** val-A D0 events with a Call A E3 state, outside the embargo. The embargo is the first 5 sessions of
  val-A from the calendar (2025-07-01 … 07-08), checked against NA-03's `embargo` flag. 8,376 events.
- **Draws:** NA-04's `states.build.stratified_sample` with the same strata (tier × month) and seed (2026), so
  the order inside a stratum is shared: identification (400) ⊂ reading validity (500) ⊂ stage 1 (700) ⊂ probe
  sample (1,000) ⊂ stage 2 (3,000). `sample.draw` asserts every inclusion; it does not assume them.
- **Consequences:**
  - the 1,000-event probe sample is exactly NA-04's unmasked twins (asserted). So probes d and e use the same
    events, and the masked side of d is stage 2's own Call A;
  - NA-07's 1,500 val-A E3 sample is inside stage 2 as well. Its E3 Call A requests for the stage-2 models
    are already cached (same state hash, same battery).
- **D2 twins:** the Call B state "W2:{company}:{session}": 3,000 of 3,000.

## Metric (S2)

- **Daily IC** = Spearman (Pearson on average ranks) of N̂ with the target, per session.
- **Minimum cross-section: 5 events.** PREREG §11.1 sets no minimum. Stage 1 has 4–24 events per session; 5
  keeps 57 of 59 sessions, while NA-02's 10 would keep 35. The value was fixed from event counts only, before
  any answer.
- **Constant days:** a session whose signal is constant has IC 0. Dropping such a day would keep only the days
  a model discriminates.
- **Summary:** the mean daily IC with its Newey–West t (5 lags); se = |mean / t|.
- **Targets:** z_cc,t (primary) and e5 (secondary), both from `panel_public`. The panel purges by split, so
  e5 is missing for the last 4 val-A sessions and y2 for the last 5; no val-B outcome is read.

## MCS (S2)

- **Statistic:** T_R (HLN §3.1.2) on loss = −daily IC. Each pair's variance comes from the bootstrap means. The
  worse model of the maximal pair is eliminated. A model's MCS p-value is the running maximum of the step
  p-values; the set keeps p ≥ α (HLN's definition).
- **Bootstrap:** stationary over days, mean block 5, 10,000 reps, seed 2026. One set of draws serves every step.
- **Days:** the sessions on which every model has a daily IC. Every model had all 57 days.
- **Check against arch:** with arch's own bootstrap indices, `mcs` reproduces `arch.bootstrap.MCS` p-values to
  1e-12, for both R and max (`tests/test_tournament_mcs.py`). arch counts T* > T and keeps p > α; with
  continuous statistics this makes no difference.
- **Robustness:** T_max is reported beside T_R.
- **Stage 2:** at most 6 survivors, highest stage-1 IC first. Ties mean ICs equal to 12 decimals; the cheaper
  model is the one with the lower stage-1 realized cost per request.

## Probes (S4)

All probe requests are separate requests. Each carries a `StateInfo.probe` tag, which the cache audit reads.

- **a. Company identification.** The masked Call A state with one choice question, "Which company is this news
  most likely about?", over 4 candidates on neutral keys `candidate_1..4`.
  - **Candidates:** the true company and 3 other S&P 500 members of the session in the same GICS sector and
    size tier. Both fields are in the state, so a candidate outside them could be ruled out by reading the
    state rather than by recognizing the company.
  - **Sector, not industry:** ATOM holds GICS level 1 only (PREREG §15 NA-01 / NA-04 rows).
  - **Small cells:** when a sector-tier cell has fewer than 3 others, it is filled from the sector by nearest
    size rank (6 of 400 events).
  - **Order and names:** a per-event seed draws the candidates and their order. Names come from
    `panel/companies.parquet` without the share-class suffix.
- **b. Month identification** (report only). The same design over 2024-07 … 2025-12: the true month and 3
  others.
- **c. Counterfactual edits** (`edits.py`).
  - **Families:** the PRD's four (beat↔missed, raised↔lowered guidance, upgrade↔downgrade, above↔below
    expectations) plus PREREG §8.3's raise↔cut.
  - **Companions:** rating ladders and NA-04's price-target template flip with them.
  - **Refusals:** every rule that can make an edit incoherent refuses the whole state instead:
    - another subject ("T-Mobile dials up a big earnings beat as it bests the company");
    - questions and previews;
    - an outcome synonym left unflipped ("topped", "surpassing", "lags", "beat and raise");
    - a bare target rating ("Upgraded to Hold");
    - a moat, outlook or credit-rating target;
    - an actual-vs-estimate number pair;
    - a stock move left by masking;
    - mixed directions.
  - **How the rules were tuned:** iteratively, on the edits, by reading samples of pairs; never on an answer.
  - **Selection:** 398 of the 8,376 pool states are editable. The 200 with the smallest
    u = sha256("2026:counterfactual:<event_id>") were taken, stage-2 events first (139 of 200).
  - **Score:** the flip rate over pairs with |N̂_orig| ≥ 0.25. A model with no such pair fails (passing must
    be shown).
  - **Files:** the full pairs (masked text) are in `work/dm/tournament/counterfactual.parquet` (gitignored).
    The committed `artifacts/NA-06/counterfactual_edits.jsonl` lists the edits without any article text.
- **d. Masked vs unmasked.**
  - **Comparison:** paired daily ICs on the 1,000 twins. The unmasked requests carry `unmasked_twin`, the
    probe registered in `configs/dm.yaml` (refusal d).
  - **Identical twins:** 7 unmasked states equal their masked twins (nothing to mask), so they share a cache
    key.
- **e. Shuffled news.**
  - **Design:** on the same 1,000 events, the `news` block of another of them. The donor comes from a seeded
    permutation with no fixed point and a different company; every other block (company, market, attention)
    is the recipient's.
  - **Coinciding states:** 1 shuffled state equals its donor's own state, because the two companies' context
    words coincide that day.
- **f. Reading validity** (report only).
  - **Request:** one core + extended request per event, for the model with the highest stage-2 Call A IC
    among those that pass every screen (the best overall if none passes). This is A2.1's production request.
  - **Main news:** the state's first news item, matched to its article through NA-04's masked title and
    snippet.
  - **References:**
    - Q8 against the vendor `tag_type`, through the fixed map in `configs/tournament.yaml`, plus the raw table;
    - `quantified` against `tag_quantified`;
    - `scheduled` against NA-04's earnings-in-W0 flag (also visible in the state as words);
    - `credibility = rumor` against `tag_rumor`.

## Screens (S4) and the hand-off (S6)

- **Screens:** `probes.screens` with `configs/tournament.yaml` thresholds:
  - company ID ≤ 0.40;
  - flip ≥ 0.70;
  - unmasked − masked ≤ 0.02 or |paired t| < 2;
  - |shuffled t| < 2.

  A missing value fails. `tests/test_tournament_screens.py` recomputes every pass/fail from
  `probe_details.csv` with thresholds parsed from PREREG §8.3 and compares them with the leaderboard.
- **Hand-off:** `allow_val_b: true` is written by a separate stage (`handoff`). It runs only after the report
  has recorded `tournament` in `configs/frozen.yaml`, and only after every NA-06 call.

## Budget

- **Dry runs:** before every sending stage, recorded in `artifacts/NA-06/dry_run.json`. The stage-1 abort
  threshold is $1.5 (PRD S2); stage 2 STOPs if its projection exceeds what the caps leave (PREREG §13).
- **Realized cost ratio:** `spend.json` holds usage.cost ÷ (catalog price × input tokens) per model.
- **Cost model:** NA-05's calibration is unchanged. The NA-05 verifier's clef-flash finding (routing at up to
  2× its catalog price) is visible in the ratio.
