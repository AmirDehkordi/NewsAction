## Analysis spec (S1)

- **Pure functions over "analysis frames".** `newsaction.analysis.spec` takes a frame with one row per event
  and readings prefixed by source (`a0_` Call A W0, `a2_` Call A W2, `b2_` Call B D2, `b1_` Call B D1, `m_`
  market) and returns every pre-registered statistic; `frames.py` only joins registered files and is outside
  the hash. Every constant lives in `configs/analysis.yaml` (hashed).
- **Hashed with it:** `inference.py` (FE OLS, two-way clustered and Driscoll–Kraay covariance), `shapley.py`
  (exact Shapley over {R, N, J}) and `metrics.py` (the bootstrap, ICs, calibration, Holm/BH). `metrics.py`
  carries copies of the three helpers it needs from earlier chunks (tests assert equality), so no analysis line
  sits outside the freeze hash (§15 row "§4.4 (2)").
- **Synthetic tests** recover planted effects (b > 0, c < 0, a Shapley interaction, a convergence path with a
  known half-life; H1 and H1b null rejection rates near 5% over 20 replications).
- **Val-B smoke run** (`production.smoke_spec`, `artifacts/NA-11/spec_smoke_val_b.json`): 29 steps on val-B,
  including the A1 ladder; the plateau is cross-checked against `car_lvl_c20` of `targets.py` (max difference 0).
  The ridge protocol's splits are shifted for the smoke (fit and λ on val-A, evaluation on val-B), so no
  val-B number feeds a choice.

hand-off asks NA-12 to report its readings as uninformative.
