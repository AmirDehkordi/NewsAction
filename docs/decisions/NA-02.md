# NA-02 decisions — news timing, member mapping, cleaning, embeddings, PIT novelty

Date: 2026-10-24. Branch: `feat/NA-02-news`. Self-audit: `artifacts/NA-02/REPORT.md`.
PREREG deviations: §15, four NA-02 rows (appended in the working tree; operator to confirm).

## Pipeline and memory

- **One stage per process.** `python -m newsaction.news.build --stage prepare | embed | articles | coverage`.
  Each stage reads the previous stage's registered file, so no process holds the corpus text, the embeddings
  and the groups at once. Peak RSS: prepare 8.1 GB, embed 5.7 GB, articles 10.4 GB, report 9.6 GB.
- **Text is read only for member rows.** `load.load_year_light` reads every column except title, snippet and
  summary. Mapping and membership run on that. `load.read_text` then streams the text batch by batch, for
  member and market-index rows only. `summary` is reduced to `summary_present` as soon as it is read.
  `body` is never read: `load.read_year` refuses it, and a test checks that.
- **Incident.** The first full run blew up the machine's memory. My scratch driver script lacked an
  `if __name__ == "__main__"` guard, so each spawned detector worker re-ran the whole stage. The
  `newsaction.news.build` CLI was never affected. The fix: the driver got a guard, the pool shrank to
  ≤ 6 workers, and `detect.detect_articles` documents the requirement.
- **Determinism.** Rerunning all four stages from scratch reproduced every sha256 in `work/news/`. The rerun
  reused the stored embeddings (`text_sha` unchanged). Re-encoding 2,000 random texts on MPS gives cosine
  ≥ 0.9992 to the stored vectors.

## Mapping (S2)

- **ATOM's ticker history is empty in practice.** `symbol_tickers` has 11 rows (6 symbols), all open from
  1990, and none of the renames named in PRD S2. Curated rows in `configs/news.yaml` supply the validity
  ranges:
  - renames: SQ→Block (to 2025-01-21), PEAK→Healthpeak, CHK→Expand Energy, FB→Meta;
  - restrictions: XYZ from 2025-01-21; NYSE:DOC is Healthpeak only from 2024-03-01, because before that it
    was Physicians Realty Trust (the vendor's 2023 DOC articles are about it);
  - share classes that are not ATOM symbols: PARAA, BF-A, LEN-B, mapped to the member class's symbol_id.
- **The vendor tags by its own ticker list.** Old tickers persist after renames: BK, MMC, FI and PARA run
  through 2026, and ATOM's history covers them. New tickers appear in the 2023 backfill (CPAY, EG, DAY, COR);
  those are the same company, so they stay open-ended.
- **Exchanges.** Only NYSE, NASDAQ, NYSEAMERICAN, NYSEARCA, BATS and AMEX count as `us_equity`. US tickers
  share one namespace, so an exchange that differs from ATOM's (PLTR and WMT after their moves) does not
  matter. The exchange rule does matter elsewhere: NYMEX:CL is crude oil, not Colgate.
- **Mapped share.** PRD S2's "member-day article volume" has no external reference. I defined it as US-equity
  articles whose ticker is any identifier of a member company (ignoring validity ranges) on a member-day of
  that company: **2,906,710 of 2,906,734 = 99.9992%**. The 24 misses are the pre-2024-03 NYSE:DOC rows. This
  share is near 100% by construction. The informative checks are:
  - the top-30 unmapped tickers (`artifacts/NA-02/unmapped_top30.csv`) are all non-members (ETFs, MSTR, RIVN,
    SNOW, SOFI, LNG, JEF, …);
  - 20 members are never covered at all (§Coverage).
- **Block.** Block is a member from 2025-07-22. Its only vendor articles are tagged NYSE:SQ, up to 2025-01;
  there is no NYSE:XYZ row in the corpus. So Block has no member-day news. AC2 is therefore tested at the
  mapping stage: every real NYSE:SQ row of news_2024 maps to symbol 1602, plus SQ/XYZ fixtures.
- **Collisions.** `mapping.check_collisions` refuses overlapping intervals for one ticker on two symbols.
  `resolve` refuses an article that matches two symbols. Both are 0 on the real data. Duplicate article ids:
  70 in the corpus, all NASDAQ/NYSE:FLYW (not a member), so 0 among member articles.

## Cleaning (S4)

- **13F regex, three rounds of judgment** (200 flagged + 200 unflagged `insider_ownership` titles each,
  seed 2026, a fresh draw each round excluding ids already judged; `spam_judgments.csv`):

  | Round | Flagged precision | Unflagged that are 13F |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 200/200 | 56/200 |
  | 2 | 199/200 | 26/200 |
  | 3 (final) | 200/200 | 13/200 |

  Rounds 2–3 added MarketBeat templates the first regex missed ("Buys Shares of 13,441 <Co>", "Acquires New
  Shares in", "Holdings Lessened by", plural possessives), GuruFocus fund-move pages, UK holdings / Form 8.3
  notices, 13F/13G filing articles and ownership templates. They also fixed three bugs: "Congress Asset
  Management" was excluded as congressional, "(NASDAQ:COO)" tripped the COO role, and a state treasurer was
  treated as a Form-4 insider. Role abbreviations are now case-sensitive and never preceded by `:`/`$`.
  Estimated recall within flagged ∪ insider_ownership: 0.99.
- **Judgment criterion.** An institutional-holdings auto-article (13F, 13G, UK holdings notices).
  Congressional STOCK-Act trades and Form-4 insider trades are "no". Journalism about a famous fund's 13F
  (Buffett, Druckenmiller) is "no". One round-1 label was revised in round 2 for consistency (tradingkey
  "Institutional Confidence" template).
- **Form 4** is a separate flag (insider roles + trade verb, Form 4 / Form 144, "Insider Sold Shares Worth",
  MarketBeat's "<Person> Sells N Shares of <Co> (EXCH:T) Stock"). Such articles are metadata, not text
  (`keep` = False).
- **`third_party_ok` = `parsing_allowed is True`.** None counts as not OK until the operator decides (REPORT
  §7).
  *Update 2026-10-26:* the operator allowed None (decision A). `third_party_ok` is now `parsing_allowed` True or
  None, never False, through `configs/frozen.yaml` `news.parsing_allowed_none: allow` (`clean.none_policy`).
  The `prepare` and `articles` stages were rebuilt (PREREG §15).
- **Committed judgment files** carry NewsWitch titles and sentences, as the PRD requires. Rows from
  `parsing_allowed == False` articles are redacted (2 titles, 1 sentence). Each row records its
  `parsing_allowed` value (`true` / `null` / `false` / `synthetic`).

## Detectors and scrub (S5)

- **after_fact** covers price-path language of any time, not only same-day: PREREG §6.3 removes "percent price
  moves" from states anyway. The rules (`configs/patterns.yaml`):
  - a stock or market subject (not "per share", "market share", "shares outstanding", "stock split",
    "stock-based") followed within 8 words by a move verb;
  - adjacent up/down ("Stock Up on …");
  - a move or up/down with a same-session phrase ("in early trading", "at last check", "intraday");
  - closed/opened/ended + direction or price; "as of hh:mm"; "trading higher";
  - gaps, moving-average crosses, new 52-week highs;
  - "<word> jumps 5%", unless a fundamental noun is among the 6 words before or a comparison period
    ("from the previous year", "y/y") follows.
- **premarket_talk** needs a pre-market / after-hours phrase **and** price content in the same sentence.
  "Reports before the bell on Thursday" is scheduling and stays. Plain percents and dollar amounts do not
  count as price content (earnings previews quote EPS).
- **scrub** removes flagged sentences, then share-price levels ("$45.10 a share", "trading at $45",
  "52-week high of $412"). It keeps per-share fundamentals, deal prices and price targets, using a
  60-character left context within the sentence. Separators between kept sentences are preserved.
  `keep_premarket=True` (for Call B) keeps pre-market talk.
- **Evaluation.**
  - Planted set: 91 sentences (`tests/data/detector_planted.csv`; 42 after-the-fact and 12 pre-market
    positives, 37 hard negatives). Recall is 1.00 for both flags and no hard negative is flagged.
  - Judged precision on 200 real flagged sentences from cleaned articles (150 after_fact, 50 premarket;
    seed 2026): round 1 was 0.94. The fixes found there:
    - word boundaries: "losing" matched inside "closing", "Advanced Micro Devices" matched "advanced";
    - "closing up to 60 stores";
    - year-over-year percents;
    - short interest.
  - The final round, on a fresh sample, is **0.945** (after_fact 140/150, premarket 49/50). The remaining
    false positives are mostly forecasts ("Will Meta Stock Hit $1,000", "Prediction: … Will Jump").
- **English only.** Non-English text is not scrubbed: the after-the-fact rate is 0.4% on non-English
  articles against 8.3% on English ones. `non_english_hint` (script or ≥ 2 distinctive function words) marks
  6.7% of kept articles. Downstream handling is an owner decision (REPORT §7).
- **Speed.** The detector runs at ~176 µs per article serially, over a spawn pool of ≤ 6 workers.

## Embeddings (S6)

- bge-small-en-v1.5 at revision `5c38ec7c405ec4b44b94cc5a9bb96e735b38267a`, sentence-transformers 6.2.0,
  torch 2.14.1, MPS, batch 256.
- **fp16 compute.** On MPS it runs 2.3× faster than fp32 (2,229 against 958 texts/s), and its cosine to the
  fp32 vectors is ≥ 0.9995 on 10,000 texts. That is the same order as the float16 storage the PRD asks for.
  `embed.compute_dtype` records it.
- All 2,906,710 member articles were embedded in 1,289 s (2,255/s). Text = title_clean + ". " +
  snippet_clean; empty parts are dropped. 30,733 cleaned articles have no text left after scrubbing; they get
  the embedding of "" and are excluded from dedupe, novelty and `keep`.
- **Storage.** The file is written with pyarrow and has no pandas metadata. pandas cannot read back the
  metadata of a fixed-size-list extension column, even when only other columns are requested. It is
  registered with `io.register_file`, and `embed.read_embeddings` hash-checks it.

## Dedupe and novelty (S7, S8)

- **Greedy anchor groups, per company, in (t_avail, id) order.** An article joins the most similar
  representative within [t−72 h, t] if cosine ≥ 0.93; otherwise it starts a group.
  - Why not connected components: a later article could merge two earlier groups and change past
    representatives, which would break AC4. The greedy groups never chain beyond 72 h.
  - Ties in t_avail are ordered by id.
- **Novelty** is measured against **strictly earlier** representatives within 7 days (`searchsorted
  side="left"`), per PRD S8. PREREG §6.5 says "articles". On 40 random companies (94,037 articles) the
  all-articles variant differs by a mean of 0.0017, 5.0% of articles differ by more than 0.01, and the
  Spearman correlation is 0.997. Logged in §15.
- **Usable set.** Both run over cleaned articles with clean text: 1,669,437 articles.
  - 10.5% are duplicates; group size p99 is 3, max 48.
  - Novelty: median 0.206, p10 0.070, p90 0.351; 0.37% have no earlier representative (novelty 1.0).
- **Counts.** `dup_count` (total group size) is `post`. `dup_count_w0` / `dup_count_w2` count members in the
  representative's own window and are PIT attention features.

## Leakage audit (S10)

- Daily Spearman IC of the ticker-day mean vendor sentiment of **kept** W0 articles. Sessions 2023-07 …
  2025-12-31. `audit.assert_public` runs before every join, and `public_targets` asserts the panel's range.
- Rule: LEAKY ⇔ IC(z_id) ≥ IC(z_on) and t(IC(z_id)) > 2. The paired-difference t is reported too.
- **Result: no cell is LEAKY.**
  - Summary present: gap IC 0.053 (t 13.5), open→close IC 0.003 (t 0.6). So `summary_variant_allowed: true`
    and `excluded_sources: []`.
  - Near miss: `retail_analysis`, with an open→close IC of 0.031 (t 3.0). It passes only because its gap IC
    is 0.056 (difference t −2.0).
  - After-the-fact W0 articles: gap IC 0.165, open→close IC 0.051 (t 5.7). These carry pre-market moves;
    they are scrubbed anyway.
