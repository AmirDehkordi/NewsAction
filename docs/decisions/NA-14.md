## How a number reaches the page

1. **Each figure reads only its CSV.** `figures/data.py` assembles one long CSV per figure from the upstream
   rows it plots; a figure module (`fig1_hero_car` … `fig8_explain_move`, `appendix`) holds `plot(df)` and reads
   no file; `render.draw` reads the CSV, draws, checks the type size and the width, and saves.
   - *Why:* the PRD asks for it (S2), and the verifier greps for other reads; a module without file access
     cannot read another source by accident.
2. **Every plotted value names its cell.** A figure row carries `source` (the upstream file), `key` (its row,
   from the file's key columns) and `y_col` / `x_col` / `lo_col` / `hi_col` / `se_col`; `sources.trace`
   re-reads each cell (`tests/test_figures_trace.py`, AC4).
   - *Why:* "no new analysis" (AC4) becomes a test. The only arithmetic is a 95% interval from a stored SE
     (±1.96·SE) or from a stored t (±1.96·|value / t|); bootstrap bounds are copied.
3. **Every caption number is a table cell.** `captions.yaml` cites `{{table/key/column@spec}}` from
   `tables/keys.json`; `[[...]]` marks a label or a design constant (t+20, 10 bp, |G| ≥ 3); names that hold
   digits (d1-ultra, NA-12, §8.3, D0, H1b) are recognized; any other digit fails `captions.check` (AC2).
4. **The paper tables.** NA-12's 20 tables are copied byte for byte (their keys are NA-12's `keys.json`); NA-13's
   strategy table and the NA-03, NA-06, NA-07, NA-11 figure sources and NA-12's figure data gain a row key ("/"
   in a model id becomes ":", since the cite syntax uses "/"); NA-12's exploratory JSON and NA-13's MA-cross JSON
   are flattened one row per object, values copied; t21, t22, t26 and t30 are records (below). One LaTeX format
   for all: booktabs, numbers in math mode with a true minus, model ids written out, long tables as longtables,
   wide ones in landscape or as a column view (the CSV keeps every cell), checked by compiling
   `paper_tables.tex` with tectonic (14 remaining overfull boxes, the largest 22 pt, in two landscape appendix
   tables).

## Choices the PRD leaves open, or where the data cannot follow it

5. **Figure 1 bands are ±1.96 event-level SE, not bootstrap bands.** The figure data hold means and SEs; a
   bootstrap would need the event-level paths, i.e. new analysis. Bands are drawn for the extreme deciles only
   and called descriptive.
6. **Figure 1's paths start at the previous close** (the PRD's rungs). The pre-event drift (t−5 … t−1) is not
   drawn; the caption gives it (d1-ultra's top decile −1.19 and bottom +1.54 from the previous close five
   sessions earlier) as NA-12's momentum caveat.
7. **Figure 1 (d), the RG classes, are pooled over tiers** (the figure data have no tier split) and drawn for
   d1-ultra only; Laya-Fin has 146 events with |N̂| ≥ 0.5 (t30, the sum of its three classes), so its noisy class
   paths are not drawn and the panel says so (operator item 3).
8. **The V deciles use a diverging blue ↔ red scale with a gray middle** (deciles 3–8): polarity is the colour's
   job. The red arm is the reference red slot at the OKLCH lightness of blue steps 450 and 650, validated as an
   ordinal ramp.
9. **Figure 3 has two panels.** Laya-Fin and the anchor are not in NA-06's tournament sample, so (a) is the
   tournament (IC against cost, the $0 local models in a strip) and (b) is val-B against test for the final
   instruments and baselines from t14, where Laya-Fin and the anchor are highlighted.
10. **Figure 4 extends the axis to the release dates** and shades "after the sample: no data". Laya's release
    date (2026-09-18) is in no artifact: it is the operator's (preamble item 3), recorded in
    `artifacts/NA-14/inputs/operator.yaml`, because `src/` may not hold a 2026 date literal (`test_blinding`).
11. **Figure 5 draws Shapley values, not shares.** Every total is negative (R² −0.0017 to −0.0013), so the shares
    of t13 are shares of nothing. There is one decision point (D2): H3's ridge fits exist only there.
12. **Figure 7's exits follow each family's hypothesis horizon** (S-CONV and S-D2 at t+5, S-D0 and S-D1 at the
    close); every other book is in t20. The placebo band is the one of Laya-Fin's universe; the tier panels are
    descriptive sub-books without a band (NA-13 owner decision 3).
13. **Figure 8 (a) is a stacked bar for d1-ultra only.** Laya-Fin's increments are negative in some tiers and the
    trained models give no Call B explanation, so (b) shows the news-only steps as dots with intervals. Roll's step
    one (market + sector, R² 0.25) is of total variance, a different base: it is annotated, not stacked.
14. **Appendix.** A1 reliability (target e5), A2 encoding (the "best" flag of NA-07's data is the pooled val-A pick,
    E1, which lost the val-B check to E3), A3 company identification plus the probes' descriptive columns
    (exploratory), A4 ablations, A5 the forest of the D0 results, A6 and A7 exploratory (post hoc) with a banner.
15. **t26 recomputes three NA-13 numbers.** NA-13 REPORT §4 states the S-CONV / gap-fade overlap (92% of positions,
    99.8% on the same side, correlation 0.96) but no committed file holds it; t26 recomputes it from NA-13's
    registered positions and books (`io.read_parquet`, hash-verified) and the test checks it against the REPORT.

## Style (CLAUDE.md F22; the dataviz skill)

16. **Palette:** the skill's reference instance, light mode, on a white print surface. The validator
    (`validate_palette.py`, run 2026-10-29) passes the first three slots on all pairs against `#ffffff` (worst
    CVD ΔE 9.2, normal-vision 24.0) and slots 5–7 on all pairs (Figure 5's groups); aqua and magenta sit below
    3:1, so their series carry legend labels and every figure ships its CSV. The neutral ink ramp and both
    diverging arms pass as ordinal ramps.
17. **Entities keep one colour:** Laya-Fin blue, d1-ultra (and the API comparators) orange, the price-only gap
    fade aqua, the anchor muted gray with "uninformative (near-constant outputs)", the vendor-label baselines dark
    gray with † and "possibly look-ahead-contaminated upper bound", span-02 with ‡ and its S5 note.
18. **Type:** DejaVu Sans (bundled with matplotlib, so the files do not depend on system fonts), 7–8 pt at print
    size; figures are drawn at 3.3 or 6.9 in, and `render.check` refuses text below 7 pt.
19. **Files:** PDF with TrueType fonts and no dates, PNG at 300 dpi without a software stamp; a rebuild writes the
    same bytes on this machine (tested), so the PDF and PNG are registered too.

## Results as found, not as expected

20. Captions follow the NA-12 and NA-13 REPORTs. Four statements of the first draft (and one of the operator's
    summary) did not match the data and were corrected before commit:
    - "every IC falls from val-B to test": only the ICs that are positive on val-B fall; the anchor, bge-ridge
      and price-only are about zero in both;
    - "the API models decline like the anchor": the anchor's monthly ICs scatter around zero throughout;
    - "every pre-registered book is negative net at 10 bp" (operator preamble item 3): every S-CONV, S-D0 and J
      book is, but Laya-Fin's four RG books (6 to 12 positions over the test) and the anchor's gated S-D2 J at
      t+20 are positive net, and none clears the placebo or the deflated Sharpe (NA-13 REPORT §1). The Figure 7
      caption says so;
    - Figure 2: d1-ultra's strong-news tercile also reverses slightly for moves of 0.5 to 2, which the caption
      states next to "smaller moves do not".
