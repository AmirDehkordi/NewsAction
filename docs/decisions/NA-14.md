## How a number reaches the page

1. **Each figure reads only its CSV.** `figures/data.py` assembles one long CSV per figure from the upstream rows it
   plots; a figure module (`fig1_validation_test` … `fig7_memorisation`, `appendix`) holds `plot(df)` and reads no
   file; `render.draw` reads the CSV, draws, checks the width, the type size and the printability of every text,
   and saves.
   - *Why:* the PRD asks for it (S2), and a module without file access cannot read another source by accident.
2. **Every plotted value names its cell.** A figure row carries `source`, `key` and the columns it took;
   `sources.trace` re-reads each cell (`tests/test_figures_trace.py`, AC4). The only arithmetic is an interval:
   ±1.96 stored SE (±1 in the exploratory monthly figure, A6), ±1.96 × |value / t|, or the Wilson interval of a
   stored share and its stored number of trials (Figure 7); bootstrap and placebo bounds are copied.
   - *Why:* "no new analysis" (AC4) becomes a test.
3. **Two simple aggregations of stored files** are NA-14 tables, each recomputed by a test: t33, numpy's quantiles
   (linear, as NA-13) of the Sharpe ratios of NA-13's 100 placebo books per universe, which also reproduces the
   quantiles NA-13's REPORT §7 quotes; t35, each test book's break-even cost, gross mean ÷ turnover, which
   reproduces NA-13's REPORT §6 (8.3, 3.7 and 5.1 bp).
4. **Every caption number is a table cell.** `captions.yaml` cites `{{table/key/column@spec}}` from `keys.json`;
   `[[...]]` marks a label or a design constant. Since the revision pass the captions are written in LaTeX notation
   (`$z_{cc}$`, `$\hat N$`, `$\kappa_h$`) and the uncited-number check reads inside math too.
5. **Every number in a LaTeX table is a table cell.** `figures/latex.py` renders cells of `keys.json` by (table,
   key, column) and lists them per table in `tables/latex.json`; a test checks each cited key exists.

## The revision pass (operator, 2026-10-29): format, figures, tables

6. **Format.** The manuscript is an arXiv LaTeX article (11 pt, US letter, 1-inch margins, one column, text width
   6.5 in). Figures are drawn at print size, 6.5 in (full) or 3.15 in (half), and placed at 100%. Type is Computer
   Modern, as in a default LaTeX document: matplotlib's bundled `cmr10` with mathtext `cm` and math-formatted ticks
   (true minus signs). Text is 8 pt, nothing below 7 pt, bold only for panel letters. cmr10 follows TeX's OT1 layout,
   so `< > | _ ^ { } \ ~` and non-ASCII print wrong: every symbol and piece of notation is math, and
   `style.plain_text_ok` (checked by `render.check`) refuses anything else, which also rules out raw underscores.
   PDF (TrueType fonts embedded, no dates) and 300-dpi PNG, byte-stable. This replaces the first build's DejaVu
   Sans and 3.3 / 6.9 in widths.
   - *Deviation from the dataviz skill's typeface:* the skill's default is a sans; its typeface is a design-system
     parameter, and the operator set it to the manuscript's Computer Modern.
7. **The main figure set, built around the findings** (operator item C). Seven figures, numbered in the order the
   paper shows them: 1 validation against test (the dumbbell of t14, grouped, † on the vendor-label baselines);
   2 priced at the open (IC with $z_{on}$ beside $z_{id}$, and H1); 3 convergence (d1-ultra's extreme deciles
   against their dashed targets; $\kappa_h$ of Laya-Fin and d1-ultra with the line at 1 and the half-life "not
   reached within $t{+}20$"); 4 over- and under-reaction (d1-ultra's Under / Fair / Over paths with each class's
   mean $V$; the drift by $|G|$ per news-strength tercile); 5 explaining moves (the news steps on their own scale,
   then the explanation step); 6 strategies (gross and net Sharpe against the placebo's 5th–95th range; S-CONV
   against the gap fade); 7 memorisation (company identification with Wilson intervals against the matched
   baseline).
   - *Numbering:* the operator listed strategies as 5, memorisation as 6 and the optional explaining-moves figure
     as 7, but the outline puts explaining moves (§5) before strategies (§6), as the tables do (T5, T6). LaTeX
     numbers figures by appearance, so the files follow the paper: `fig5_explaining_moves`, `fig6_strategies`,
     `fig7_memorisation`.
8. **The appendix** (A1–A15, in the order of the sections): the tournament (rows sorted by IC with cost beside,
   so no label overlaps); the filters; the encodings; reliability; robustness; the monthly IC with ±1 SE bands
   (exploratory: the implied SE is NA-12 §13's); every decile of $V$ and $\kappa_h$ with the anchor; Figure 4 (b)
   for Laya-Fin; Shapley as one compact dot plot (pooled over tiers, $R^2$ and IC); Call B ablations; persistence
   by move reason with $\theta$; the other books' paths and S-CONV by tier; the probes' descriptive columns
   (exploratory); where the D0 signal lives and the MA cross (exploratory, post hoc).
9. **Figure style** (operator item B; the dataviz skill). Short panel titles; explanations in the captions, none in
   the plot area. Lines labelled at their ends where they separate, else a small legend outside the data. Interval
   lines 0.7 pt or bands at 15%, markers 4 pt with a white ring, light horizontal gridlines only, no top or right
   spine. Event-time axes say "not to scale"; $+k$ is the close of session $t+k$ (the captions say so). Tags such as
   "(model-specific)" and footnotes moved to the captions; † and ‡ stay on the names.
10. **Colour** (the skill's reference palette, light, on white). One colour per model: Laya-Fin slot 1 (blue),
    d1-ultra slot 2 (orange), the gap fade / price-only slot 3 (aqua); every other model, the placebo and the
    references are context greys. The validator passes the three on all pairs against white (worst CVD ΔE 9.2,
    normal vision 24.0); aqua's 2.8:1 contrast is relieved by direct labels and the CSVs. Ordered classes of one
    model take its ordinal ramp: Laya-Fin blue 250 / 450 / 650; d1-ultra orange at the OKLCH lightness of blue 250,
    the orange slot itself, and orange at blue 550's lightness (`#ff8653`, `#eb6834`, `#a82800`), both validated
    with `--ordinal` (monotone L, adjacent ΔL ≥ 0.06, light end ≥ 2:1). The method reproduces the first build's
    red arm (`#d2383a`). Components (R, N, J, the ladder's steps) are encoded by position, so no colour is spent on
    them and the "muted component set" is not needed.
11. **The anchor** is uninformative and stays out of the main figures; it appears in Figures A4, A7, A9, A13, in
    Tables 1–5 beside the primary, and is named in the captions where relevant.
12. **Figure 4's terciles are on |V|** (NA-12's `a2_v`, Call A's value on the day's news), not the PRD's |N̂|.
    NA-12's figure data hold that split only (`figdata.drift_by_g(…, "a2_v")`), and a split on |N̂| would be new
    analysis. A presentation deviation; the figure's legend and caption say "tercile of $|V|$" (fix pass).
13. **Tables** (operator item D). Six main tables, `tab1_primary` … `tab6_strategies`, each drawn from several CSVs:
    T1 the primary hypotheses with the anchor beside the primary (the tests' statements moved to the notes, to fit
    the width); T2 the IC with $z_{cc}$, $z_{on}$, $z_{id}$ on val-B and test; T3 b and c with their SEs by decision
    point; T4 $\kappa$ by rung, $\lambda$ and the half-life; T5 the ladder's increments and H5; T6 gross and net
    Sharpe, turnover, break-even cost, placebo percentile and DSR (positions left out, to fit the width). Every
    other CSV the paper shows has an appendix table. Style: booktabs rules only; threeparttable notes for the
    floats (a long table is a longtable whose notes follow it, because threeparttablex's notes overflow a
    longtable's foot); siunitx S columns (`group-digits=integer`, so decimals are not grouped); IC 3 decimals, t 2,
    p 3 or "<0.001"; SEs in parentheses, CIs in brackets; panels with \cmidrule; captions above with a one-line
    takeaway and a label; human headers; no internal key in the visible text (a test strips math and comments and
    finds no underscore). Wide appendix tables are landscape.
14. **Val-B ICs with $z_{on}$ and $z_{id}$** (T2) come from NA-11's run of the frozen spec on val-B, before the
    freeze (`spec_smoke_val_b.json`, flattened as t32). It covered the trained and zero-shot Laya models only, so
    the API models' cells read "not computed"; computing them now would be new analysis.
15. **Pre-registration deviations** (t34): one line per row of PREREG §15 (63 rows: 56 before the freeze, 7
    after), date and timing read from the log, chunk, change and reason summarized by NA-14 in
    `inputs/prereg_deviations.yaml` (checked row by row, and at most 12 / 58 / 34 characters, so the table fits one

16. **The LaTeX preview** (operator item E). `paper_preview.tex` (written by `make paper`, digest enforced) holds

17. **Exploratory material is appendix-only, and checked** (operator check 5e). `figures.guard` lists every main

## Choices the data decide (first build, still in force)

18. **SE bands, not bootstrap bands** (Figures 3, 4, A7): the figure data hold means and SEs; a bootstrap would need
    the event-level paths. Bands are descriptive; captions say "SE bands" (owner decision 5).
19. **The RG classes are pooled over tiers** (Figure 4 a; the figure data have no tier split) and drawn for d1-ultra
    only; Laya-Fin has 146 events with |N̂| ≥ 0.5 (t30), too few to draw.
20. **Laya's release date** (2026-09-18) is the operator's, recorded in `inputs/operator.yaml` with its source
    (owner decision 2), because `src/` may hold no test-year date (`test_blinding`).
21. **Shapley values, not shares**: every total is about zero, so the shares are shares of nothing (A9).
22. **t26 recomputes three NA-13 numbers** (the S-CONV / gap-fade overlap) from NA-13's registered positions and
    books, and the test checks them against NA-13's REPORT §4.
23. **Results as found:** captions follow the NA-12 and NA-13 REPORTs; the first draft's four misstatements and the
    operator's "every book negative net" were corrected (Laya-Fin's RG books and the anchor's gated S-D2 J at t+20
    are positive net; owner decision 1).
