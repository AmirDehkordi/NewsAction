## Metric and bootstrap

- **Daily IC:** NA-06's: Spearman of N̂ with z_cc,t per session, ≥ 5 events with both values; a constant
  signal gives 0 (`tournament.metrics.daily_ic`). The mean has a Newey–West t (5 lags).
- **Bootstrap:** stationary over sessions (block 5, 10,000 reps, seed 2026; `tournament.mcs.stationary_indices`).
  There is one index draw per split, on the split's sample sessions, shared by every series of that split. A
  session a series lacks is missing in its bootstrap mean. SE = standard deviation of the bootstrap means; CI =
  the 2.5–97.5% percentiles.

## Rules

- **Encoding (val-A):** within each model, rank the five encodings by IC (1 = highest; ties to 12 decimals share
  the average rank; a missing IC ranks last). The best encoding has the lowest mean rank. A tie that includes
  E3 → E3; any other tie → the higher mean IC, then the lower index (`study.choose_encoding`).
- **Val-B check:** only when the best is not E3. Δ = mean over sessions of the per-session mean over models of
  IC(best) − IC(E3), on the sessions where all six daily ICs exist; keep the best iff Δ ≥ −1·(bootstrap SE of Δ)
  (`study.confirm`). When the best is E3 the check is vacuous ("keep best unless it is worse than E3"), so no
  val-B state is sent and `run.targets` refuses to read any val-B value.
- **Numeracy:** a (news, direction) series over the 7 magnitudes is monotone iff P(too_large) + P(much_too_large)
  is non-decreasing and P(too_small) + P(much_too_small) is non-increasing (tolerance 1e-9); a missing answer
  makes the series non-monotone. The share is per model over 60 series; the target is 0.90. The synthetic
  block uses NA-05's grid and canary values: S&P 500 +0.1, volume 2× ("busy"), "by the close". E0 and E4 would
  need a raw change and a percentile. These are z × 1.5% and the normal percentile of |z|; both increase with
  |z|. They were not needed, since the probe ran in the chosen encoding.
- **Anchoring:** per model, OLS N̂_B = a + b·G + c·N̂_A + e (b = slope) and Pearson corr(N̂_A, N̂_B). A model
  anchors iff b ≥ 0.30 or corr < 0.70. Anchoring is present iff any model anchors; `frozen.yaml` records the
  largest b and the smallest corr, so §8.4's rule on the recorded pair gives the same verdict.
