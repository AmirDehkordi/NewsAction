## Choices the frozen text leaves open (all in the §15 NA-13 row)

1. **The returns.** NA-01's grid through `panel.build.assemble` (not a re-implementation), so r_id, r_cc, SPY's
   legs, β_m and the volumes are the panel's own.
   - The public build equals `panel_public` on 9 shared columns; the test build equals the public build on val-B
     (17 columns) and `targets_test` on r_cc, r_spy_cc and ar_cc, all to 0.0.
   - *Why:* the trades must use the same returns as the frozen targets, and a second implementation could drift.
2. **The SPY hedge.** β_m of the entry session (252 sessions ending the session before). The sector part of the
   abnormal return is not hedged.
   - *Why:* §12 says "β-hedged with SPY"; no tradeable sector instrument opens with the stocks (NA-01).
3. **Calendar time.** Each decision's cohort holds 1/L of the capital (L = holding sessions), with equal weights
   restored daily; costs are charged at entry and exit only.
   - *Why:* the usual Jegadeesh–Titman convention; A1.6 and §12 ask for calendar-time portfolios.
4. **The 20% sides and the pooling rule.** k = ⌊0.2·n⌋. A tier with k < 10 joins the session's pool; a pool still
   below 10 next to a tier that stands alone makes the whole session one group.
   - *Why:* "pooled if a side has < 10 events" names the deficient side's tier, not the session. The fallback
     avoids trading a 4-a-side pool next to a 12-a-side tier.
5. **Ties.** Ñ breaks on N̂ (Ñ is a monotone step function of N̂, so this is ranking by N̂), then on a seeded
   uniform draw in event-id order.
6. **J and RG have a direction.** §7 defines J relative to the move so far (too small or too large) and RG
   relative to the news direction (RG = (N̂ − G)·sign(N̂)). The books therefore rank the implied drift: sign(G)·J
   and N̂ − G for |N̂| ≥ 0.5, with N̂ from Call A as in NA-12's RG classes.
   - *Why:* an unsigned J would buy a "too small" rise and a "too small" fall alike.
   - Owner decision 1 in the REPORT.
7. **Gating.** S-D0 (D0 gate) and S-D2 (D2 gate) only.
   - A1.6 carries over §12's costs, DSR and benchmarks for S-CONV, not the gate.
   - S-D1 has no frozen gate (NA-11).
8. **The D1 fill.** The 10:00 bar's closing price (§2.5), stamped one second after the 10:00 decision, so the
   timing guard's strict inequality is meaningful and an entry at the decision instant is refused.
9. **Validation = val-B only.** The V and Ñ maps and the gates were fit on val-A, so val-A books would use their
   own outcomes.
10. **The gap fade** is −G^lvl at D1 and D2, the S-CONV rank with V = 0.
    - *Why:* the price-only counterpart of the primary strategy, and NA-12 found U ≈ −G^lvl.
    - At D0 there is no reaction before the open, so there is no gap fade there.
    - The vendor-sentiment rank runs at D0, where its readings live.
11. **The placebo** draws 100 uniform signals per rule's universe; it shares the universe with every rule on the
    same events (universes keyed by point, exit and the event set).
    - Rules sharing a universe read their own signal by event id; an earlier draft let them share the first rule's
      column, and that was caught before any result was used.
12. **The DSR.**
    - Trial variance: ATOM's analytic default, because the other ledger trials are not Sharpes.
    - N: the ledger's whole count when the DSR is computed (after every NA-13 row).
13. **Trials.** One "strategy" row per (rule, split) with a position; metric net Sharpe at 10 bp.
    - Cost levels and placebo seeds are not rows: they are accounting and the null, not variants a researcher
      selects among.
    - `run trials` is idempotent: an identical row is skipped, a different value raises.

## Findings that shaped the reporting (not the books)

- **Net-Sharpe percentiles against the placebo are volatility-driven** when costs dominate: (μ − c)/σ is less
  negative for more volatile books. The REPORT leads with the gross percentile and adds the mean-return
  percentile as a labelled, descriptive supplement written after seeing this.
- **S-CONV ≈ gap fade.** It shares 89–96% of its positions with the gap fade, and the daily returns correlate
  0.93–0.98, the uninformative anchor included. Hence U ≈ −G^lvl carries over from NA-12's λ to the trades.
- **The blinded raw prices end on 2026-07-29.** The last two test sessions cannot be entered at the open; up to
  419 events a book are dropped as "no entry price" (disclosed in REPORT §3).
