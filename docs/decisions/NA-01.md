## Export (S1)

- **Credentials.** `NewsAction/.env` did not exist. The export received `ATOM_DB_PASSWORD` in its process
  environment, read inline from `POSTGRES_PASSWORD` in `~/GitHub/Atom/.env`. The value was never printed or
  written to a new file. To re-run the export, put the variable into `.env` (operator checklist).
- **Read-only.** One SQLAlchemy session; its first statement is
  `SET TRANSACTION ISOLATION LEVEL REPEATABLE READ, READ ONLY`, so the whole export reads one consistent
  snapshot. `SHOW transaction_read_only` = `on` is recorded. Counts of `md.price_eod` and `uni.member` are
  taken in separate read-only transactions before and after, and inside the snapshot. All four are equal:
  2,016,915 and 1,551. The session is rolled back.
- **5-minute `statement_timeout`.** `md.price_adjusted_total` runs correlated subqueries per row, so one call
  for 566 ids timed out. Prices are fetched in chunks of 25 ids, all inside the same snapshot.
- **Estimates.** These are change-driven snapshots with `known_from` = `as_of_date` and no restatements.
  `get_estimates` is called once per month M, 2023-01 … 2026-07, with `as_of` = the last instant of M and
  `snapshot_start` = the first day of M. Each ticker's resolved id was checked against our symbol_id
  (lowest id per ticker): none aliased.
- **Sealed files** are written by the export (ATOM venv) and registered by hash only (no parquet footer
  read). Each gets a `write` entry in `ledgers/test_access.jsonl`, the same record `lock.write_sealed`
  leaves.
- **ATOM imports** sit inside the script's `main`, so its pure helpers are tested in the NewsAction env.

## Mapping and adjustment (S2)

- **Mapping validation uses Spearman ≥ 0.99** on at least 120 non-event days, not Pearson: GNRC's correct
  mapping has Pearson 0.981 because of one bad ATOM print. Pearson is stored and reported. A wrong mapping
  has Spearman well below 0.9.
- **Coverage.** 525 of 565 ids are mapped. The other 40 have no candidate at all (Data/prices download
  failures or names never requested); the obvious guesses SW→SWK and SNDK→WDC are different companies. These
  40 use ATOM total-return closes (PREREG §15).
- **SPY** uses ATOM raw OHLC plus the `work/market/market_prices.parquet` close (PRD S2).
- **QC.** Flagged values are never changed; the panel carries `qc_flag` / `qc_rule` so later chunks can
  exclude them.

## Sector, risk (S3–S4)

- **Sector pool.** One representative class per member company, leave-own-company-out, at least 5 other
  companies. Pool membership for 2022 sessions, which only feed the first betas, is the 2023-01-03
  snapshot.
- **Betas** come from rolling moment sums (one `.shift(1)`) and match `numpy.lstsq` to 1e-9 (synthetic) and
  1e-6 (MSFT).
- **Sigmas.** The MAD uses exact medians per window (np.median for full windows, nanmedian otherwise).
  The cross-sectional floor is applied before the half-day scaling. Every sigma below the floor is raised to
  it, so on a given day the floor equals the minimum member sigma. This matters for the hand
  recomputation: MSFT on 2025-06-12 sits at the floor (raw MAD 0.00645 → 0.00676).
- **z_vol** needs at least 40 of 60 sessions (PREREG gives no minimum). **ADV63** needs at least 40 of 63.

## Tiers (S5)

- **Ranking universe.** Members at the ranking session are ranked, giving exactly 100 / 150 per month. A
  company joining during month M is tiered by comparing its cap with the rank-100 / rank-250 caps
  (`joiner_threshold`). A ranked company with neither a cap nor DV gets `no_data`: 3 company-months, all in
  2026, for new spin-off listings without public prices.
- **Representative class**: the higher 63-session ADV ending at the ranking session; ties go to the
  smaller symbol_id.
- **Fallback.** A company without shares gets the cap of the company with the nearest log ADV: 553
  company-months in total, at most 16 per month.
- **AC2 wording.** "tier for 2025-03-17 changes" cannot hold, because March tiers use the February
  ranking. The test checks the PIT property instead: every March 2025 tier is unchanged, and April's ranking
  inputs move.

## Targets and reactions (S6)

- `targets.py` and `reactions.py` do their arithmetic with numpy/pandas plus `calendar`, `config` and
  `lock` only, so nothing outside the NA-11 freeze hash can change a target.
- **Test period.** `build_targets(…, "test")` and `build_reactions(…, "test", …)` first call
  `lock.read_sealed` for both blinded price files. Before the freeze or unblinding this raises `LockError`
  and logs the refusal. After that, the frames go to `build.test_returns_panel`, which rebuilds the panel
  with the same pipeline. This route is tested in a throwaway repository with a real `lock.freeze()`
  record.
- **Public routes** refuse any test-period row (`LockError`), as does the placebo helper.
- **Scaling.** Mixed-window z scaling, unclipped multi-day z, delisting hold and the `warmup` split are
  set out in PREREG §15 row 6. Clean-window flags (NA-03) and the 10:00 rung (NA-10) are not built here.
- **Exploratory rung.** `car_lvl_c60` is computed and purged like every other rung; it is mostly NaN in
  val-A/val-B by construction.

## Build (S7)

- **Panel rows.** panel_public keeps member sessions plus priced sessions up to 60 after an exit (the
  longest rung). Without this cap, SBNY's years of OTC prints after its 2023 exit dominated the panel's
  non-member rows.
- **Test inputs omit `sigma_on` / `sigma_id`**: they need blinded opens. Call A (D0) states need only
  close-based features; NA-11 rebuilds the rest through the lock.
- **Determinism.** The build is deterministic (two runs gave identical sha256) and takes about 15 s.
