## S1: what the unblinding reads, and why

- **Frozen routes.** `targets.build_targets(…, "test")` (the S1 route the PRD names) gives the daily outcomes.
  `build_d1_targets` gives the D1 ones. `reactions.build_reactions` at D2 and D1 gives G and the 10:00 rung,
  exactly as the NA-11 Call B states were built.
- **Same-day z.** `build_targets` returns forward targets only. The same-day z (z_cc, z_on) live in NA-01's panel,
  and `test_returns_panel` exposes AR, σ and z_id but not them. So the run reads the blinded prices once more,
  in the same sequence, and applies `panel.risk.zscore`, the function NA-01 computed the public z with. The
  rehearsal confirms the result equals `panel_public` on val-B; on test, g_d2 = z_cc exactly.
- **The day-t 30-minute path.** Figure data, using NA-10's `derive.compute` per bar. Its first point is the
  frozen 10:00 rung, checked on val-B (max 0.0) and asserted on test (0.0). The alternative was a val-only
  intraday figure. Reading the bars inside the one sequence, with a checked first point, kept the figure on the
  test sample at no extra access.
- **Guards** (each with a test and a mutation that turns it RED):
  - `require_unblind` first, so a missing variable or a hash mismatch refuses before any logged read;
  - refusal after any "targets" entry or when the output exists, so the sequence cannot run twice;
  - consistency gaps that must be 0.

## Findings that shape NA-13 and NA-14

1. **Laya-Fin's test IC fell to a third of its val-B value** (+0.020 vs +0.065). Every primary hypothesis is
   "not supported"; H5 is the only surviving secondary test (q 0.058).
2. **The API models price the news at the open, not after it.** H1 = +0.075 with p 0.0001 for d1-ultra, span-02
   and drex-v2.0, robust to every variant but the "latest article before 04:00" stratum. H1b = 0 for all.
   S-D0 has no pre-registered support; S-CONV's premise (closing the price–value gap) has none either (λ ≈ 0).
3. **No convergence after day t.** Gap closure measures the reversal of G, because V is small next to G^lvl
   (U ≈ −G^lvl); the λ are nearly identical across models.
4. **Vendor-label baselines lead the test ICs** (tags-GBM 0.055, sentiment 0.047). They are retrospective labels
   (CLAUDE.md A6) and may carry look-ahead: present them as upper bounds.
5. **All models' monthly IC collapses in June–July 2026.** Worth a descriptive data check before NA-13; the API
   models were released after the whole test period and show the same pattern.
6. **The anchor is uninformative**, as NA-11 warned. Its nominal H3 p (0.017) comes from a uniform continuation
   head. The operator's anchor robustness runs after 2026-10-31 address it.
7. **H3's ridge chose λ = 10⁴ everywhere**, and the full model's OOS R² of y2 is ≈ −0.002, so the Shapley shares
   are shares of nothing.
