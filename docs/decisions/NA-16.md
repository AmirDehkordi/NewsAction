## What the paper claims, and where the operator's summary was adjusted

The paper states the verified results of NA-03 … NA-14 and NA-13b. Five points of the prompt's summary were
checked against the REPORTs and the keyed tables and are written differently:

1. **"News from 04:00 to 08:00 ET is the most informative" (exploratory list).** No REPORT or table supports
   it. The pre-registered timing split (NA-12 §11 (b), operator row before NA-11; `t16_robustness`) shows
   d1-ultra's overnight IC largest when the latest article arrives 08:00–09:15 (0.115, t 5.0), then 04:00–08:00
   (0.061), and not significant before 04:00 (0.024, t 1.1); Laya-Fin's IC with z_cc is 0.006 before 04:00 and
   0.025 in both later windows. The paper reports this split as a pre-registered robustness result in Section 5,
   not as an exploratory finding.
2. **"For every model, the predictive power sits in the overnight gap."** True for the commercial models and
   Laya zero-shot (H1 0.054, p 0.001); for Laya-Fin both test ICs are small (0.015 with z_on, 0.010 with z_id)
   and H1 is not supported. The paper says that where the models predict anything it is the overnight gap, and
   that no model predicts the move after the open.
3. **"Non-generative models that return calibrated probabilities"** (abstract draft). The commercial models are
   overconfident (finding 6), so the abstract says "return probabilities"; the introduction says the vendors
   describe them as calibrated.
4. **The return split.** PREREG §5.1 defines simple returns with r_on = (1 + r_cc)/(1 + r_id) − 1, so Equation
   (1) is written as (1 + r^cc) = (1 + r^on)(1 + r^id), with "in logs, the close-to-close move is the sum" in
   the text, instead of the prompt's additive form in log returns.
5. **NA-14's own caption of the strategies figure** said that gross, every book lies inside its placebo range.
   Laya-Fin's S-D2 J at t+5 sits at the 3rd percentile, below it. The paper says so (Section 9), and the NA-14
   caption template is corrected (`captions.yaml`, Figure 6 of the preview).

Smaller points: "the API models' ICs roughly halved" is NA-12's own wording (d1-ultra 0.097 → 0.039, span-02
0.099 → 0.041, drex-v2.0 0.075 → 0.044); "no pre-registered strategy survives costs" is written as "survives
costs and selection" (no book clears its DSR or its placebo's 95th percentile), because Laya-Fin's one-name RG
books and the anchor's gated S-D2 J at t+20 are positive net of 10 bp (NA-13).

## Choices

1. **Spelling and terms.** American English throughout ("memorization", as in the keywords); one term per
   concept: "decision model", "pre-open reading" (Call A), "post-reaction reading" (Call B), "convergence
   strategy" (S-CONV), "news-implied move" (N̂), "news-implied value" (V). The strategy labels S-D0, S-D1, S-D2
   and S-CONV, which Table 6 and Figure 7 print, are defined once in Section 9.
2. **Notation.** Equations use superscripts with indices (r^on_{i,t}, z^k_{i,t}), the tables and figures
   subscripts (z_on); Section 3 says so once. D0, D1, D2, z_cc, z_on, z_id, N̂, V and G are defined in Section 3,
   and the notation table (Appendix B) repeats them with the equations they enter.
3. **Figure numbers follow first mention.** Section 8 (memorization) precedes Section 9 (strategies), so NA-14's
   `fig7_memorisation` prints as Figure 6 and `fig6_strategies` as Figure 7; the selective-prediction figure
   (`figA16`) is Figure 8 in the exploratory section. `make paper-pdf` checks the order and that every float is
   cited.
4. **The attenuation result (Equation 6)** is labelled an interpretation, not a test, with its assumptions
   (classical error independent of V* and of the regression error after the fixed effects; a common set of
   events for the shape result), cited to Hausman (2001).
5. **Exploratory labelling.** Every sentence of Section 10 that cites a number says "exploratory" or "post hoc"
   (`make paper-text` checks it); the disclosure of the assistant's earlier looks at the test is one sentence in
   Section 10 and one in the captions of Figure 8 and Figure A3. Laya-Fin's IC at its frozen confidence gate is
   left out (operator's option).
6. **AI-assistance statement.** The paper carries a visible placeholder for a statement on the use of AI tools,
   with suggested wording, for the authors to confirm; the paper does not claim what the authors reviewed.
7. **The withdrawn paper.** Kim, Muhn and Nikolaev (arXiv 2407.17866) was withdrawn by its authors (v3, 20 Feb
   2025); it is not cited.
8. **Published versions.** Lopez-Lira and Tang and He, Lv, Manela and Wu are cited as their 2026 JFE articles,
   Glasserman and Lin as JFDS 2024, ModernBERT as ACL 2025, C-Pack (BGE) as SIGIR 2024 with its published title.

## Numbers: the macros, the registry and the allowlist

- `paper/numbers.yaml` maps 270 names to cells of `artifacts/NA-14/tables/keys.json` with NA-14's citation
  syntax; `make paper-text` renders them with `figures.keys.render` into `paper/generated/numbers.tex`
  (`\res{name}`; an unknown name stops the compile).
- **Keys added in NA-14's generator** (`tables.derived`, table t30, each row with its formula and source): the
  tournament's models (21) and second-stage models (6), from NA-06's `fig3_data.csv`; the conditioning study's
  pairs (75), selections (8), selections negative (5) and positive (3) on the test, from NA-13b's
  `selection.csv` (marked exploratory rows, so no main caption or table may cite them); the ablation requests
  that were rate-limited and not retried (210 = Σ requests − ok over NA-11's `ablation_runs.json`, a new input of
  `make paper`).
- `paper/allowlist.yaml`: the phrases and patterns that may hold digits by hand, each with its reason: dates,
  years, clock times, hypothesis and decision-point names, model names, and design constants fixed by the
  pre-registration (windows of 252 and 126 sessions, the 1.4826 scale, 10,000 bootstrap draws, Holm at 0.05,
  BH at 0.10, the 30% filter budget, the screens' 40% and 70%, the 700/3,000-state tournament samples, the 5, 10
  and 20 bp costs, the quintiles and deciles, the level midpoints, seeds 13, 17 and 23). In math, whole numbers
  pass (indices such as t+20 or 1/2); a decimal must be an allowlisted phrase.
