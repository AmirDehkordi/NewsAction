Date: 2026-10-27. Branch: `feat/NA-09-baselines` (worktree `NewsAction-NA09`, cut from `main` @ `7bc114b`; NA-07,
NA-08 and NA-10 ran in parallel in their own worktrees). Self-audit: `artifacts/NA-09/REPORT.md`. PREREG

## Package layout (`src/newsaction/baselines/`)

- `__init__`: `configs/baselines.yaml` → `BaselinesConfig`; paths; `log_trial` (a record is never logged twice).
- `schema`: the common prediction columns, `frame` and `validate`; `FitRecord` and `check_fit_record` (AC1).
- `data`: the event frames, the state's articles (rebuilt and checked), the input checks, the targets.
- `fitting`: fit / tune rows, IC and R², the val-A probability maps, `save_model`.
- `sentiment`, `embed_ridge`, `gbm` + `tags_gbm`, `price_only`: the fitted baselines.
- `laya_zeroshot`: the S6 run (cached, resumable) and its predictions.
- `llm_baseline`: the S7 model rule, sample, prompt, run and predictions.
- `metrics`: the S8 table (val-A only).
- `run`: the stages (`python -m newsaction.baselines.run --stage laya | articles | fit | llm_sample | llm_select
  | llm_dry | llm_run | laya_predictions | llm_predictions | metrics | report`).

`dm/client.py` gains `chat_json` (below); `dm/schemas.py` the chat request and response schemas; `dm/costmodel.py`
a completion price and `estimate_chat`; `configs/dm.yaml` the chat and credits endpoints and the chat-model price
snapshot; `configs/availability.yaml` four NA-09 columns.

## The common schema (S1)

- Columns: `event_id, model, call, split, session, company_id, N_hat, P_up, P_down, mag_hat, y_hat, extra`. The
  PRD's nine plus three:
  - `session` and `company_id`, so NA-11 can join and compute daily ICs without another file;
  - `y_hat`, because price-only D2 predicts the subsequent drift y2, which is not the eventual move N_hat stands
    for.
- `call` = "A" for a D0 event with a Call A state, "B" for a D2 event with a Call B state. The event set of every
  baseline is the set of states, so all baselines and the decision models answer on the same events.
- `validate` enforces: exact columns; finite numbers; P in [0, 1] with P_up + P_down ≤ 1; mag_hat ≥ 0; `extra` a
  JSON object; no duplicate (event_id, model, call); every row's event, split, session and company equal a state
  row of its call; no call-B row in the test period; test rows only for sentiment, embed_ridge and tags_gbm
  (AC2, AC3). `run.write_predictions` validates every file before writing it.

## The state's articles

The news baselines read the articles a decision model reads:
- A D0 state holds the first `n_articles` of its event's W0 list (by rank) that NA-04 did not leave out (move
  explainers, TradingView reposts, non-English articles, running market stories) and that kept text after
  masking. `fit_news` drops articles only from the end.
- `data.rebuild_state_articles` rebuilds the ids that way. It checks every state's news items against the masked
  title and snippet (the first item may be cut by words).
- All 54,332 Call A states match: 189,705 article rows (`work/baselines/state_articles.parquet`).

## Inputs and the availability registry

- Every source column is checked with `pit.check_inputs` at its decision point (D0 for the news baselines and
  price-only D0, D2 for price-only D2).
- The vendor fields (sentiment, intensity, tag type, the five flags) are `selection_only` (CLAUDE.md A6).
  PREREG §10.1 and §10.3 pre-register them as baseline signals, and the PRD notes they were produced live.
  `check_inputs(..., vendor=True)` admits exactly that list; other `selection_only` columns (`source_class`,
  `keep`, the learned filter scores, `event_age_days`) stay refused. The source classes come from
  `source_class_domain` (`w0`), not from the vendor-adjusted `source_class`.
- Registered in `configs/availability.yaml`:
  - `z_vol_lag1` (t−1): z_vol of the previous session;
  - `react_z_on` (d2_reaction): z_on,t used as a D2 input. The registry says that same-day quantities used at
    D2 need their own column;
  - `embedding` and `text_sha` (w0).

## Fitting and tuning (S2–S5)

- **Rows.** `fitting.fit_rows` = train with a target; `fitting.tune_rows` = val-A with a target, outside the
  embargo. Targets come from `panel_public`, purged by split, so e5 ends 2025-06-24 in train and 2025-09-24 in
  val-A, and y2 ends one session earlier. Test sessions are absent there.
- **Fit records** (`work/baselines/models/<model>_<target>.json`) = {record, model, prob_maps}:
  - the fit range, tuning range and calibration range are computed from the rows used;
  - the selected settings and the whole grid with its val-A scores;
  - the seed and the library version;
  - the model itself: isotonic thresholds, OLS or ridge coefficients with the standardization, or LightGBM's
    model text.

  `check_fit_record` refuses any range outside its split before anything is written.
- **Selection metric.** For N̂ and y2: the mean daily IC (≥ 5 events a day, Newey–West t with 5 lags, as NA-06)
  with the fitted target. For mag_hat: the R² of |z_cc| against the train mean of |z_cc|. Ties go to the first
  configuration in grid order (ridge: the smaller λ).
- **Sentiment (§10.1).** The mean of −2 … +2 over the state's articles. N_hat = isotonic (increasing, clipped)
  e5 on that mean. mag_hat = OLS of |z_cc| on the share of non-neutral articles and the share with intensity
  Immediate or Short-Term. No tuning.
- **Embedding + ridge (§10.2).** The mean of the state articles' bge vectors (384) plus log1p of the five
  attention fields. Missing values take the train median. Standardized with train statistics. λ ∈
  {1e−2 … 1e6} (9 values).
- **Tags + LightGBM (§10.3).** 47 features:
  - tag counts (15 types), the five flag shares;
  - sentiment mean, min and max, share non-neutral, share timely, intensity shares (5);
  - source-class counts (6), novelty max and mean, the W0 duplicate count, the number of state articles;
  - the five attention fields, tier and sector (categorical).

  Grid: num_leaves {7, 31} × min_child_samples {50, 400} × n_estimators {200, 800}, learning rate 0.03,
  subsample 0.8, colsample 0.8, seed 2026, deterministic, 4 threads.
- **Price-only (§10.4).**
  - D0: z_m5, z_m20, z_m60, z_vol of the previous session, the σ regime (σ_cc percentile vs its own 252
    sessions), tier and sector → e5 and |z_cc|.
  - D2: the same plus G = z_cc,t, z_on,t and z_vol,t (PREREG §11.5's R) → y2.
  - Same grid as the tags model.
- **P_up / P_down** of every fitted baseline: isotonic maps of its N̂ to 1[e5 ≥ +0.5] and 1[e5 ≤ −0.5],
  fit on val-A outside the embargo. This is the §7 calibration rule; the range is stored in the record. Where the
  two would sum above 1 they are scaled down together. Val-A probabilities are therefore in-sample, and no
  probability metric is reported for them here.
- **Determinism.** A rerun of `fit` writes the same predictions and logs no new trial (258 lines before and after
  the rerun).

## Test-period predictions

- **Made here:** sentiment, embed_ridge and tags_gbm on the 13,977 test Call A states. Their inputs are the
  state's articles (vendor fields, embeddings, novelty), t−1 counts, tier and sector; no 2026 price or return is
  read.
- **Not made here:**
  - Price-only D0 needs z_vol of the previous session, a 2026 volume. `panel_test_inputs` carries only the
    60-session volume median and MAD, and the raw 2026 volume is sealed. Only 2026-01-02, whose previous session
    is 2025-12-31, has the value.
  - Price-only D2 needs the 2026 reactions.
  - NA-11 predicts both after the freeze with the saved models
    (`work/baselines/models/price_only_*.json`; LightGBM text).
- **Laya zero-shot test Call A** runs in NA-11 after the freeze, like S7's test subsample. NA-05's client refuses
  every test-period state before the freeze, local models included (rule (b)); that guard was not touched.

## Laya zero-shot (S6)

- **Scope (operator, 2026-10-27).** All splits:
  - Call A D0: val-A 8,906, val-B 6,035, train 22,621, robustness 2,793;
  - Call B D2: val-A 11,148, val-B 7,828, train 29,221.

  That is 88,552 requests, 720 already cached by NA-05 and NA-06. Run order: val-A A, val-B A, val-A B,
  val-B B, train A, train B, robustness A.
- **Runtime.** Measured before the start on this Mac (MPS, fp32):
  - one request at a time: 0.516 s per Call A state, 0.582 s per Call B state;
  - laya's `predict_batch` with 4 / 8 / 16 states per pass: 0.62 / 0.68 / 0.77 s per state, slower, with
    bitwise-identical float64 probabilities;
  - two processes at once: 1.24 s per state each (0.62 s combined), slower than one.

  The projection was 13.4 h at batch size 1, and the operator chose all splits. In the run, the GPU was shared
  with the desktop (a Chrome renderer at ~100% CPU) and other worktrees, so the rate was ~0.73 s per request
  (~18 h).
- **Path.** `DecisionClient("NA-09", concurrency=1)` with the core battery, so the refusals and the cache are
  NA-05's. The 700 NA-06 stage-1 requests are cache hits (same model, state, questions and checkpoint
  settings). The run is resumable: each answer is cached as it arrives.
- **Predictions:**
  - N_hat = N̂, P_up / P_down = Σ p over Q1's rise / fall levels, mag_hat = E|N̂|;
  - `extra`: M, surprise and tone (Call A) or J, W and C (Call B), the nine Q1 probabilities, the build, and
    laya's truncation flag.

## The generative LLM (S7)

- **`chat_json`** shares every guard of the decision path:
  - refusals (a)–(f) and the pilot rule. Eligibility is read from `frozen.yaml` `chat_models`; a decision model
    is not a chat model and a chat model is not a decision model; there is no local chat backend;
  - the same sqlite cache. The key payload carries `endpoint: chat` and the whole body (model, messages, schema,
    temperature, seed, max_tokens, provider), so it can never collide with a decision key;
  - offline `CacheMiss`; the batch projection check before any send; the per-call reservation and record; the
    retries and the semaphore.
  - The provider object can only be `{"data_collection": "deny", "require_parameters": true}`.
    `require_parameters` routes the request only to endpoints that honour the JSON schema, seed and temperature.
  - The answer parser is strict: `finish_reason` "stop", one JSON object with exactly the schema's keys, numbers
    in [0, 1] summing to 1 within `sum_tol`.

  `tests/test_chat_json_refusals.py` covers each rule online and offline on a mock transport (0 transport calls).
- **Model rule** (`configs/baselines.yaml` `llm.selection`, fixed before any call). Candidates in the live
  OpenRouter catalog (463 models; sha256 in `llm_catalog.json`) must be:
  - general-purpose instruct models from their original developers (OpenAI, Google, Meta, Mistral, Qwen,
    DeepSeek, Microsoft, Amazon, Cohere, Anthropic), with no guard, coder or vision variant, no `:free`, no
    `:batch` and no alias;
  - text in and text out, ≤ $0.30/M input;
  - with `response_format`, `structured_outputs`, `seed` and `temperature`, and not a reasoning model;
  - with a published knowledge cutoff before 2024-07-01, the train start, so no sample-period news is in their
    pre-training data.

  Matched cost: the pick is the candidate whose projected cost per 1,000 Call A decisions is closest to the
  median realized cost of the 19 NA-06 API decision models ($0.0422 per 1,000 Call A requests). The projection
  takes the sample's mean state bytes plus the prompt bytes / 4 at the prompt price, plus 120 output tokens at
  the completion price. Ties go to the later release.

  Result: **mistralai/mistral-nemo** ($0.029 / $0.03 per M; cutoff 2024-04-30; projected $0.034). The next three
  were mistral-small-24b-instruct-2501 and llama-3.1-8b-instruct (both $0.062), then phi-4 ($0.090).

  If no endpoint had accepted `data_collection: deny` with `require_parameters`, the next model in the order
  would have been taken. That was not needed: the first probe was answered by provider DekaLLM.
- **Sample.** 2,000 val-B D0 events with a Call A state, outside the embargo (2025-10-08 … 12-31), stratified by
  tier × month with NA-04's `stratified_sample`, seed 2026.
- **Prompt.**
  - A fixed system message: an equity analyst reads a masked JSON description and answers with a probability
    per option, as a JSON object with exactly the given keys.
  - The user message: the state's canonical JSON (the bytes the decision models receive), Q1's instructions
    verbatim, and the nine levels as `key: level text`. Keys come from the level text (`very_large_fall` …
    `very_large_rise`).
  - The strict json_schema holds nine required numbers, each with its level as the description.
  - temperature 0, seed 2026, max_tokens 300.
- **Parse rule.** Sums within 0.05 of 1 are renormalized and flagged; anything else is invalid, with no re-asking.
  These are verbalized probabilities, not token probabilities (PRD risk note). Invalid answers are left out of
  `llm.parquet` and counted in `llm_summary.json`.
- **Credit.** Before the first paid call: the account had $10.32 left (credits $15, usage $4.68) against a
  projection of $0.155. The NA-09 cap stays $2.00.
- **Run.** One probe, then 1,999 calls at concurrency 8: all 2,000 answered (DekaLLM 1,771, Parasail 140,
  DeepInfra 89), $0.038884 ($0.0194 per 1,000), ledger − cache 0.0. Results:
  - 1,978 valid, 234 of them renormalized;
  - 22 invalid: 21 sums outside 1 ± 0.05, 1 `finish_reason` "error", 1 not JSON.
- **Parser fix, disclosed.** The first parse marked 176 more answers invalid whose sums were exactly 1.05 or 0.95.
  In floating point, 0.4 + 0.4 + 0.25 is 4e-17 above 1.05, which broke `|Σ − 1| ≤ 0.05`. The rule as recorded
  ("within 0.05 of 1") includes them. `parse_chat_response` now compares with `sum_tol + SUM_EPS` (1e-9), as the
  decision-model bound 0.0105 builds in float noise; tested, and the mutation is RED. The answers are re-parsed
  from the cached raw responses, so no call was repeated. The cache's stored `valid` flag of those rows keeps the
  first verdict.

## Val-A metrics (S8)

- `metrics.evaluation_rows` keeps the tuning split (val-A) and raises for any other split. No val-B or test metric
  is computed (`tests/test_baselines_guards.py`; mutations "metrics on any split" and "metrics read val-B rows"
  are RED).
- Per baseline and call, overall and by tier:
  - N_hat's daily IC with z_cc,t and with e5, and the R² of e5 against zero;
  - mag_hat's R² of |z_cc| against the train mean and against zero, and its daily IC with |z_cc|;
  - price-only D2's y_hat IC with y2 and its R² against zero;
  - Laya Call B: J and C with y2.
- The LLM's events are val-B, so it has no NA-09 metric. NA-11 evaluates it with val-B.
- Trials: every λ and grid point (`baseline_grid`, 58 records: ridge 9 × 2, tags 8 × 2, price-only 8 × 3) and every final metric over all tiers
  (`baseline`), logged once.

## Mutations

26 mutations were applied with a scratchpad script, each with `__pycache__` cleared, then restored byte for byte;
all 26 are RED.

| Area | Mutations |
|---|---|
| fit ranges | embed_ridge fits on train + val-A (the PRD's); `fit_rows` takes val-A; `tune_rows` keeps the embargo; tags_gbm tunes on val-B; `check_fit_record` off; probability maps fit on val-B |
| metrics | any split allowed; val-B rows read |
| schema | call-B test row allowed; any model in test; duplicates allowed; events not matched to states |
| inputs | every `selection_only` column allowed; no pit check; Laya test split allowed |
| chat_json | eligibility from the decision list; a local model allowed; test-period guard off; offline sends; no batch projection check; no reservation; cache read off; provider `allow`; key ignores the seed; sum rule off; the sum rule's float margin removed |

The first pass left two survivors: duplicates were also caught by the event match, and the projection check by
the per-call reservation. A true-duplicate test and a cap that fits one call but not three now kill them.
